Install SUAAMS on Android.

Version 0.1.0 · test build

The app is still in testing, so it installs from this page rather than the Play Store. The download is about 45 MB, so use Wi-Fi if you can.

You need an Android phone and your matric number. Phones with NFC check in with a tap; phones without NFC use Bluetooth instead.

On an iPhone? The iPhone app isn't out yet. Your lecturer can mark you present by hand until it is.

  1. Download the app

    Download SUAAMS

    Open the file when the download finishes. Older or budget phone and the install fails? Try the version for older phones.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks once whether your browser may install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.

    If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap More details, then Install anyway. It warns about any app that doesn't come from the Play Store.

  3. Sign in on your own phone

    Your account locks to the first phone that signs in to it, and a phone holds one student's account. Changing phones later means asking an admin, with your school ID.

    Sign in with your matric number and the first password your department gave you. The app makes you choose a new password straight away.

What the app asks for

Fingerprint or PIN
To confirm it's you before each check-in. The fingerprint stays on your phone.
NFC
To pass your check-in code to the reader when you tap.
Bluetooth
The fallback for phones without NFC: the app listens for the reader's short code.
Location
Android 11 and older only. Those versions make any app that scans for Bluetooth ask for it. SUAAMS never reads your location.
Notifications
To tell you when you've been marked, and about announcements.

If something goes wrong

"App not installed"
Try the version for older phones.
"Device in use"
This phone already holds another student's account. Sign in on your own phone, or ask an admin.
New phone
Your account is locked to your old one. Take your school ID to an admin to have it moved.
"Update required"
Your version is too old to check in. Come back to this page and install the new one over it.
